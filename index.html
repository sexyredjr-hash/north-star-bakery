Welcome to North Star Bakery

North Star Bakery is a neighborhood bakery focused on fresh, handmade breads, pastries, and cakes. We proudly serve local customers, families, commuters, and customers planning special events.

We are known for fresh breads, handcrafted pastries, and cakes prepared with care. Our bakery combines traditional baking practices with quality ingredients to create products our community can enjoy.
