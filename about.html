Our Story

North Star Bakery was created to provide fresh, handmade baked goods while becoming a welcoming part of the local community. Our bakery focuses on quality, friendly service, and products that bring people together.

Our Sourcing Philosophy

We believe that quality ingredients are an important part of creating great baked goods. We focus on selecting quality ingredients and working with suppliers that help us maintain the freshness and consistency our customers expect.

Meet Our Bakery Team

Our team includes bakers and customer service staff who work together to prepare products, assist customers, and keep the bakery welcoming and organized.

Behind the Scenes

Community Focus

North Star Bakery is proud to serve local customers, families, commuters, and event planners. We strive to make every customer feel welcome and provide products that fit everyday meals and special occasions.
